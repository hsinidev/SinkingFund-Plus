App Name: SinkingFund Plus
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: Personal Finance / Budgeting
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free 3 funds + $4.99/month Pro

Description:
A budgeting tool built entirely around creating, tracking, and celebrating individual sinking funds — separate savings goals for future known expenses like car repair, vacation, or Christmas gifts.

Key Features:
- Create unlimited named sinking funds (Car Repair, Holiday, New Laptop, etc.)
- Set target amount and target date → auto-calculates monthly contribution needed
- Log contributions manually or link to bank (plaid integration)
- Visual progress bars and circular fill animations for each fund
- Confetti celebration when fund reaches goal
- Withdrawal tracking with optional notes
- Total savings overview dashboard
- Monthly contribution reminders
- Fund categories: Emergency, Annual Bills, Travel, Gifts, Home, Vehicle, Medical

Target Audience: Budget-conscious adults 25-45, Dave Ramsey followers, YNAB users
Competitors: YNAB, Qapital, Digit (discontinued)
Unique Value: 100% focused on sinking funds — no full budget complexity
